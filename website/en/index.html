The basis

Where your data actually stays

These points summarize the Privacy Policy (Turkish, with an English summary at the bottom) — read the full text for details.

Statements and transactions stay on-device

The PDFs you upload and the transactions extracted from them stay in the app's database on your phone; they are not sent to a server.

Only account info lives on the server

To let you sign in, your name and email are stored on account infrastructure hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt).

No ads, no trackers

There is no ad network or behavioral tracker inside the app; subscriptions are handled through Google Play Billing.

Starting with one bank

The first phase supports a single bank; more will be added one by one. Supported banks are listed inside the app.

Family plan keeps everyone's data local

On the family plan, premium access is shared with up to 4 people; each member's financial data stays only on their own phone.

Delete your account anytime

You can permanently delete your account and server records from Settings. See Account & Data Deletion.

FAQ

Common questions

Is my statement sent to a server?

No. Your credit card statement and payslip PDFs are processed only on your phone. The server only stores your account name, email and subscription status.

Which banks are supported?

The first phase supports a single bank; more will be added one by one. The current list is shown inside the app.

What can I do on the free plan?

You can upload 1 document per month, and use manual income/expense entry, goals and asset tracking without a cap.

How does the family plan work?

The family annual plan shares premium access with up to 4 people. Each member's financial data stays only on their own phone and is not shared with the rest of the family.

When will the app be public?

FinScout is currently in internal testing on Google Play. This page will be updated once a public release date is set.
