What is FinScout?

FinScout is a personal finance app that runs on your phone and brings the hidden lines of your statement and payslip into view.

You upload credit card statement and payslip PDFs; FinScout pulls out interest, late interest, BSMV, KKDF, card fees and EFT/FAST charges separately and totals them by month and year. It checks the statement against the bank's period total and tells you if it doesn't match. Your files are processed on your phone and never sent to a server.

I only ever saw one total at the bottom of my statement. In FinScout I saw interest, BSMV and the card fee separately; now I know why some months cost me more.

Six fee types, one by one

The "total" on your statement hides several different charges. FinScout lists each one; you see monthly and yearly totals under Analysis › Fees.

Interest. The real cost of debt.

Contractual interest charged on the unpaid balance.

FinScout shows: how much interest you pay each month and per year.

Late interest. The price of being late.

Extra interest added when a due date is missed.

FinScout shows: which period you were late and what it cost you.

BSMV. Tax on interest.

Banking and insurance transaction tax, added on top of interest and some fees.

FinScout shows: BSMV as its own line, monthly and yearly.

KKDF. The invisible fund levy.

Resource Utilization Support Fund, calculated on interest.

FinScout shows: KKDF separately from interest, on its own line.

Card fee. Your yearly membership.

The annual or periodic fee charged for your card.

FinScout shows: when the fee was charged and how much.

EFT/FAST charges. Small but frequent.

Transaction fees charged on money transfers.

FinScout shows: how many transfers you made and what they cost in total.

How FinScout calculates your fees

The numbers are not estimates; they come from your own documents. Each card explains what FinScout extracts from a document and how.

Credit card statement

Interest and charges line by line.

  • Interest and late interest: split per period, totalled per year.
  • Taxes: BSMV and KKDF on separate lines.
  • Charges: card fee and EFT/FAST transfer fees.
  • Instalments: which instalment you are on and what's left.

Period total check

Every statement comes with its proof.

  • Comparison: the calculated total against the bank's period total.
  • If it matches: you see "matched the bank's period total".
  • If not: you get a warning listing which document doesn't add up.

Payslip

From gross to net.

  • By year: gross, net, tax and social security charts.
  • Raise detection: pay changes on the payslip are flagged.
  • Filters: year range and period comparison.

Goals and assets

See your savings in the same place.

  • Goals: set an amount, add contributions, track progress.
  • Assets: gold, currency, cash and vehicles in one list.
  • Monthly need: how much to set aside each month to reach a goal.
